Illustrative linguistic QA sample

This is an original, synthetic example created to show how I assess meaning in AI-assisted translation. It is not taken from a client project or confidential material.

Source · English

“The product may be available in selected stores.”

The modal verb expresses possibility, not a confirmed availability statement.

Draft · Brazilian Portuguese

“O produto estará disponível em lojas selecionadas.”

The future indicative changes the degree of certainty and turns a possibility into a promise.

Reviewer assessment

Meaning shift · modality

The translation introduces certainty that is absent from the source. This is a semantic accuracy issue, not merely a stylistic preference.

Suggested revision

“O produto pode estar disponível em lojas selecionadas.”

This version preserves the uncertainty expressed by “may”. Final wording should still follow the client’s terminology and style guide.

Review dimensions demonstrated: source-to-target meaning, modality, terminology, Brazilian Portuguese naturalness and consistency with project instructions.
